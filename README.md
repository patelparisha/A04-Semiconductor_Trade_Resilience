# A04-Semiconductor_Trade_Resilience

## Project Description:
This project provides a comprehensive, data-driven examination of global semiconductor trade flows and the structural vulnerabilities embedded in today’s chip supply chain. Using UN Comtrade and Electronic Trade Observatory datasets, we analyzed export–import patterns across major economies, identified countries central to wafer fabrication, IC manufacturing, and equipment production, and mapped how trade dependencies evolve across technological segments.

The analysis highlights the high geographic concentration of key semiconductor capabilities, uncovering where bottlenecks and geopolitical chokepoints emerge—particularly in lithography equipment, advanced fabrication, and material inputs. Through exploratory data analysis, visualizations, and comparative trend profiling, the project quantifies supply-side risks, evaluates exposure across regions, and identifies resilience gaps for both upstream and downstream actors.

We further applied scenario-based reasoning to understand the implications of disruptions—such as export controls, regional conflicts, or supply-side shocks—on trade balances and network stability. The project concludes by outlining strategic pathways countries and firms can pursue to enhance semiconductor supply-chain resilience, including diversification, capacity building, and strategic trade partnerships.
